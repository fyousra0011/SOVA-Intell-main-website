
*Design a multi-page professional event website for SOVA Intelligence — a Malaysia-based AI transformation company. The website should attract business partners, government stakeholders, potential sponsors, and the general public.*

*Draw strong visual inspiration from the design language of cinetica.studio — specifically its full black background, large cinematic editorial typography, parenthetical section labels, bold statement text between sections, horizontal logo scrolling strip, numbered process section, and minimal email-style contact form. Translate this visual language into a corporate AI company context rather than a creative studio context.*

*BRAND IDENTITY*

*Primary colours: Black and white as the base. Introduce one accent colour creatively — consider electric blue, deep purple, or sharp emerald or you may use purple gradients instead of greyish designs — to signal AI and innovation without overusing it. The overall feel should be: bold, intelligent, minimal, forward-looking. Think less startup, more institution with edge.*

*Typography: Use a strong editorial serif or geometric sans-serif for headlines — large, confident, and commanding. Use a clean readable sans-serif for body text. Mix weights dramatically between headlines and body copy the way Cinetica does.

For the logo SOVA Intelligence: Create a custom uppercase geometric sans-serif wordmark that reads "SOVA". Do not use an existing font—draw each letter as a custom vector. The typography should be extremely bold (approximately 900 weight) with uniform monoline strokes and smooth rounded corners throughout. Use a modern AI-tech branding aesthetic with clean geometric construction.

Letter specifications:

S: Thick geometric shape with completely flat horizontal terminals at the top and bottom instead of curved endings. The center connection should be broad and balanced with rounded internal corners.
O: A perfect circle with consistent stroke thickness all around and a large circular inner counter. No oval distortion.
V: Wide stance with thick diagonal strokes. The bottom vertex should be softly rounded rather than sharp while maintaining strong symmetry.
A: A custom geometric uppercase A with no horizontal crossbar. The interior should form a centered triangular counter. The legs should be thick and slightly flared with a rounded apex.

Maintain consistent stroke thickness across all letters. Apply slightly tight optical kerning so the letters feel unified as a premium technology logo. The overall appearance should be minimal, futuristic, bold, and highly polished. Use solid white lettering on a black background. Avoid gradients, shadows, outlines, textures, or decorative elements. The final result should look like a professionally designed custom AI startup logo rather than a standard typed font.
Prioritize geometric precision over readability. The logo should feel handcrafted with subtle optical adjustments instead of using a commercial typeface. The proportions should resemble a blend of Gilroy Heavy, Circular Std Black, and Gotham Rounded Ultra, but with custom modifications to the S and a crossbar-less A so it is not recognizable as any existing font. Aim for a premium minimalist brand identity similar to OpenAI, Anthropic, Linear, or Stripe.*

*Tone: Professional authority combined with human warmth. This is not a tech conference website. It is a milestone event for an AI company that works with governments and communities.*

*GLOBAL DESIGN RULES ACROSS ALL PAGES*

*Background: Deep black — #0a0a0a — for primary sections. Use #111111 and #1a1a1a for subtle contrast between sections. Never use flat white backgrounds.*

*Section labels: Always use parenthetical labels like (The Milestone) or (Who We Are) in small uppercase tracking — this is a direct reference from Cinetica's design language.*

*Bold statement text: Between every two sections, place a full-width bold statement line — a short provocative or inspiring sentence — styled large and confident. These act as visual and emotional transitions.*

*Buttons: Two styles — filled accent colour for primary CTA, outlined white for secondary CTA. Rounded corners. Generous padding.*

*Cards: Dark surface — #1a1a1a or #222222 — with subtle border — 1px #333333. Slight border radius. Clean internal spacing.*

*Hover states: All interactive elements should show a clear hover state — colour shift on buttons, lift effect on cards, underline on links.*

*Mobile: Stack all grid layouts to single column. Navigation collapses to hamburger menu. Typography scales down proportionally but maintains dramatic size contrast between headline and body. Touch targets minimum 48px.*

*Spacing: Generous. This is not a busy website. White space — or in this case black space — is intentional and communicates confidence.*

*Imagery: All image areas should be clearly marked placeholder boxes with labels describing what goes there — for example "CEO Photo", "Event Venue", "Partner Logo", "Event Montage Thumbnail." You may use stock images.*
